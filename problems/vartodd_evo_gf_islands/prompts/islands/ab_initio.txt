## Required Island Regime: Ab-initio search

Start from `Evaluator(path_name="init")` and construct a complete reusable
path. Use standalone TOHPE as the main early action source. Keep TODD disabled
or light until lower-rank execution evidence supports a scheduled transition.
Do not load or infer a saved path.

The main compute strategy is cheap action generation across the easy and middle
descent so the run can test more policy vectors. Use TOHPE as the primary early
source and keep TODD disabled or light until reached-band evidence shows TOHPE
acceptance or pool fill collapsing. Put the saved cost into optimizer
evaluations, suitable population size, independent seeds, and restarts whose
placement is justified by segment yield. Introduce heavier TODD only through a
rank schedule targeting an evidenced hard lower band.

Compare two explicit sampling hypotheses. The more-complete sampling strategy
can make each policy evaluation less stochastic and scoring more
representative. The lighter stochastic sampling strategy can fund more
optimizer evaluations and independent chances to discover a good path which is can be very
helpful especially due to generally low optimization budget and iregular function.

Seeds for policy testing are not only for final validation. Using multiple seeds during 
optimization can expose different stochastic action sets and trajectories and make the loss more robust, helping 
the search cross mapping/path plateaus and increasing the chance of discovering a strong path. 
This remains a trade-off: more seeds improve path diversity and robustness but reduce 
optimizer evaluations or per-policy action-generation depth under a fixed budget.

Inspect optimizer family, initialization, population and social/differential
parameters, termination, parameter-group staging, and restart placement. Form
one causal budget-reallocation hypothesis and keep schedules inside rank bands
that the execution can actually reach. Choose what policy parameters to optimize,
what to fix and what ranges use.
