## Required Island Regime: Medium-margin path refinement

Load one exact `path_name` from the compact mid-margin Selectable Shared Paths
inventory. Never substitute `path_name="init"`. Choose the branch point by its
quantile in the loaded path's rank span from final rank to initial rank. As a
general working region, use a margin of roughly 1/4 to 3/4 of
`initial_rank - final_rank`. This preserves enough of the useful prefix while
branching early enough to build a genuinely different descent instead of
replaying the inherited tail. Treat the branch quantile as a search variable,
not as a fixed or conveniently rounded margin inherited from prior programs.
Success means a final rank strictly below `loaded_path_rank`; equal rank is
failure even with a lower TODD cap or shorter runtime.

The inventory states the current frontier once, followed by exact path name,
final rank, and origin. All displayed paths have the same route-local failure
allowance; frontier paths receive no permanent placement or retirement
exception. Choose from the displayed evidence. A higher-rank path is useful
when it supports a concrete alternative-prefix hypothesis whose target is
below the inventory frontier. Branch before the inherited failure band, not
inside the already-collapsed tail.

Reconstruct the easier prefix cheaply with TOHPE. Schedule heavier TODD only
over reached bands where source acceptance collapses or TOHPE stops supplying
positive actions. Allocate optimizer evaluations, seeds, and restarts from
observed segment yield: productive segments may be extended, while flat rank
quantiles, repeated best ranks, and late last-improvement evidence call for a
different restart or search allocation.

Independent seeds are not only for final validation. Using multiple seeds during 
optimization can expose different stochastic action sets and trajectories, helping 
the search cross mapping/path plateaus and increasing the chance of discovering a strong path. 
This remains a trade-off: more seeds improve path diversity and robustness but reduce 
optimizer evaluations.

After a failed refinement, materially change at least one causal component:
the loaded path, the branch quantile, the source schedule, optimizer flow,
action-generation breadth, scoring, beamwidth, or path coverage. Do not submit
a cheaper replay of the same path as progress.
