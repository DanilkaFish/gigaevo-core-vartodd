## Required Island Regime: Medium-margin path refinement

Load one exact `path_name` from the compact mid-margin Selectable Shared Paths
inventory. Never substitute `path_name="init"`. Let `selected_path_rank` be the
card's `rank`. Size the branch margin from the global search span
`INITIAL_RANK - selected_path_rank`; a useful starting region is roughly 1/4
to 3/4 of that span. `producer_start_rank`, the `_i...`
component of a path name, `loaded_rank`, and a parent's local starting rank are
diagnostic values and must not replace `INITIAL_RANK` in this calculation.
Treat the branch location as a search choice rather than copying a convenient
constant. Success means a final rank strictly below `loaded_path_rank`.

The inventory has two independent sections. `Ab-initio roots` exposes up to
four strong original paths. Each stays visible after descendants improve it,
shows `uses=.../6`, and reports `best_descendant_rank` across its whole lineage.
`Mid-margin family frontiers` exposes up to four best current descendants from
distinct mid-margin families. `family_uses=.../8` is shared by every attempted
child in that family; an improving descendant replaces the representative but
does not reset the family counter. Choose the section and path that support the
experiment you intend to run. Use `best_descendant_rank` and `child_yield=i/f`
to distinguish paths that have produced improvements from paths that are merely
low-ranked and unused.

Concetrate on the scoring function choices, try different ideas and preserve what works better.
Allocate optimizer evaluations, seeds, and restarts from
observed segment yield: productive segments may be extended, while flat rank
quantiles, repeated best ranks, and late last-improvement evidence call for a
different restart or search allocation.

Independent seeds are not only for final validation. Using multiple seeds during 
optimization can expose different stochastic action sets and trajectories, helping 
the search cross mapping/path plateaus and increasing the chance of discovering a strong path. 
This remains a trade-off: more seeds improve path diversity and robustness but reduce 
optimizer evaluations.

After a failed refinement, materially change at least one causal component:
the loaded path, the branch quantile, the source schedule, optimizer flow,
action-generation breadth, scoring, beamwidth, or path coverage. Do not submit
a cheaper replay of the same path as progress.
