## Required Island Regime: Near-end refinement

Load one exact current `path_name` from the Selectable Shared Paths inventory
with an absolute margin of 5–50 ranks. Never substitute `path_name="init"` or
a historical parent handle. Success means a final rank strictly below
`loaded_path_rank`.

Small margins can produce broad plateaus because many parameter vectors replay
the same inherited tail. Use the execution evidence to choose where within the
5–50 range to branch and whether to change optimizer initialization, seeds,
restarts, or trajectory traversal. Flat rank quantiles, repeated best ranks,
and an early last improvement indicate that the current policy is saturated
and should not simply be repeated. Due to low number of possible generations higher beamwidth
instead of testing many policies can be the wining move.

Near the end of the descent, positive actions may be rare and TODD may be the
main productive source. When the diagnostics show action starvation, increase
z-bucket research, source retention, final-pool size, or `actions_per_bucket`
so that newly discovered alternatives survive merging.

Large z budgets improve rare-action recall. At terminal dimension 1–3,
the complete nonzero y space contains only 1–7 vectors; after it is saturated,
spend the remaining budget on z buckets, retained actions, or path traversal.
Choose this allocation from observed action yield and rank improvement rather
than from inherited constants. A high `limit_bucket` (including `limit_bucket=-1`) is very useful here.
