## Required Island Regime: Near-end refinement

Load one exact current `path_name` from the Selectable Shared Paths inventory
with an absolute margin of 5–50 ranks. Never substitute `path_name="init"` or
a historical parent handle. Success means a final rank strictly below
`loaded_path_rank`.

Here small margins (5-50) for loaded paths can produce broad plateaus because many parameter 
vectors replay the same inherited tail. Use the execution evidence to choose where 
within the 5–50 range to branch and whether to change optimizer initialization, seeds,
restarts, or trajectory traversal. Flat rank quantiles, repeated best ranks,
and an early last improvement indicate that the current policy is saturated
and should not simply be repeated. Due to low number of possible generations 
higher beamwidth instead of testing many policies can be the wining move because the 
number of actions can be within 10-100 per single iterations so wide beamsearch can effectively
search all the tree.
